How errors are made오류 양상 REP·MIF·OM·ADD

Four ways to make an error: REP — swap in a wrong word (에 → 에서), MIF — right word, wrong form (지속적인 → 지속적일), OM — leave something out, ADD — insert something extra. UNKNOWN = the real corpus didn't record which. Left chart: our sampling plan copies the real mix almost exactly. Right chart: the final text leans MIF, because every real UNKNOWN still had to become one concrete type when generated. 오류 4가지: REP=다른 말로 잘못 교체, MIF=맞는 말의 잘못된 형태, OM=빠뜨림, ADD=불필요한 추가. UNKNOWN=실제 코퍼스 미표기. 왼쪽=설계(거의 동일), 오른쪽=실현 결과(미표기분이 MIF로 실현되어 쏠림).

Method & caveats · 방법과 한계

Sources. NIKL = Phase 2 canonical stats (ERROR_COMBINATION_PROBABILITY_BASELINE, pair/area/level/pattern CSVs; 235,842 errors, 110,880 errored sentences, 7,748 essays). SonGul = full finalized stream of [FINAL]_PHASE5BF_PARAGRAPH_ERRORS_20260728.jsonl — the 20260714 base after tail injection, strict NIKL gap-fill, and 43,784 independently judged zero-pair replacements (1,436,790 errors, 944,175 errored sentences, 1,186,269 total sentences, 612,029 records).

Metrics. Shares use each dataset's own error total as denominator. TV = total-variation distance on the full union support. Cosine on share vectors; Spearman ρ on shared actionable canonical morpheme pairs within the real top-50; same-surface annotation artifacts are excluded. Overall replication score = unweighted mean of the eight axis similarities shown in the scorecard (1−TV for distribution axes, mass coverage for tag combinations, ρ for error words). Equal weights — no axis is privileged; the per-axis rows stay the ground truth.

Caveats. Tail injection now places errors on non-first sentences; density is still lighter than NIKL by proficiency design. The real corpus leaves 60% of levels and 42% of patterns untagged — the sampler mirrors those UNKNOWNs, but realization must pick a concrete pattern, which drives the realized-MIF skew. Engine mix makes very frequent pairs slightly over-crisp (rule edits repeat exact strings).
